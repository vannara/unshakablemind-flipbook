The story

From Reactive to Unshakable

All my life, all I knew about success was a comfortable life, a steady job, and a great salary. To get that, I had to work hard by sacrificing my freedom and happiness. No one in my circle taught me otherwise. I had to learn it the hard way.

I left a stable job in France and came home to Cambodia for freedom and happiness with my two-year-old daughter. I was a high achiever and a single mother, building my first business, when the country was hit by a crisis I did not see coming. Pressure from every side: time, money, stability. I worked nonstop, the same way I had in my corporate job. My nervous system was always on. I burned out. Worse: I became the kind of mother I hated myself for being. I screamed when I was annoyed and when she did not listen. I promised not to do it again. I kept doing it.

So I stopped building completely. I could not let her inherit this reaction. I was less reactive, but the same trigger still ran me.

The story

From Reactive to Unshakable

That led me to the patterns we inherit. Our body remembers what our mind does not. I studied how reactions work, and how we rewire them. The way I worked, the beliefs I carried, the reactions I made were inherited and conditioned. When I reprogrammed my mind and regulated my state, everything shifted. I became less and less reactive, until the same trigger did not shake me. The vision I want now manifests without force.

I generated my first income one month after launching my community. I got funded on my vision within four months of setting the intention. The state we hold is the foundation of everything we build.

This book is that protocol. Not a new personality. A daily way back to the state that can hold the life you want.

How to enter

You are here to bring executive mode back online as the room you live from — not to collect insight.

Executive mode: prefrontal control (planning, inhibiting impulse, choosing), available when the nervous system is not in fight, flight, or freeze.

Flip the corner, the arrows, or the keys. Each turn should sound like paper. The body learns: we are leaving reaction and entering creation.

Use it daily. Prime before you plan. Close before you sleep. When the loop starts, interrupt it on the page, not in the spiral.

Why it works

The science, in brief

How to use it

Five moves. One feeling.

You do not do nine things. You notice how you feel, and you make one move.

Tense — Calm down. Head too full — Empty the mind. You don't know what matters — Choose what matters. Pulled away — Protect it. Spiraling, or the day still open — Return to now.

Each move has one or two steps behind it. That is the protocol. You only need the move the feeling is asking for.

One feeling. One move. Repeated until it is who you are.

Move 1 · Calm down · 00 Prime

Waking after the world is already awake.

The day starts in a hurry. The phone decides before you do.

Otherwise. Smile. Breath. Write the power statement. Move. Then the phone.

Stress switches off the thinking brain. Prime in the waking window, before the day takes it.

Sit with thisWho chose the first ten minutes this morning — you, or the phone?

Move 2 · Empty the mind · 01 Empty

The whole day still in your head.

Overload. You cannot see what to focus on, so you pick what is loudest.

Otherwise. Write every task out. Do not rank.

Unfinished tasks keep looping until they have a place. Writing them down stops the loop.

Sit with thisWhat is circling right now because it has nowhere to live?

Move 2 · Empty the mind · 02 Sort

Every item treated as equally urgent.

You say yes to negotiables and quietly drop the vow.

Otherwise. Two columns. Non-negotiable. Negotiable.

Most overwhelm is unsorted loyalty, not too much work.

Sit with thisWhich negotiable have you been treating as a vow?

Move 3 · Choose what matters · 03 Vision

Starting the day with no vision and no focus.

Anything that arrives gets a yes, because you never decided what matters.

Otherwise. See tonight before you spend the morning.

The nervous system will not hold a fog. It needs a scene.

Sit with thisWhat did you say yes to today that did not belong to the day you meant to live?

Move 3 · Choose what matters · 04 Decide

Thirty things chosen. None of them done.

Nothing moves the needle. Force replaces choice.

Otherwise. One vision. One purpose. Three actions.

The mind cannot focus on too many things. One vision and three actions move the needle.

Sit with thisHow many things were you trying to hold this morning?

Move 4 · Protect it · 05 Protect

The important work left in the gaps.

Other people's urgency spends the hours you meant for the dream.

Otherwise. Put the three actions on the calendar. Phone out of the room.

What is not on the calendar does not get a nervous system.

Sit with thisWhere did the work that builds your life actually live yesterday?

Move 4 · Protect it · 06 Execute

Five things open, and the switching called progress.

Every switch costs focus. The one thing stays undone.

Otherwise. One action. If the state drops, return to Prime, then the same thing.

The mind is faster on one thing than on five half-things.

Sit with thisWhat were you avoiding by staying busy?

Move 5 · Return to now · 07 Interrupt

Rehearsing what you cannot control.

That hour does not go to the dream. The loop becomes the day.

Otherwise. Can I control this? Yes — next action. No — release. Part — only your part.

Acting on what you can control, and releasing what you cannot, is how stress stops spending you.

Sit with thisWhat did you spend the last hour on that was not yours to carry?

Move 5 · Return to now · 08 Close

Going to bed still worried, rehearsing the unfinished task.

You rehearse the stress in alpha, and you deepen the feeling of falling behind.

Otherwise. Drop the weight. Body scan. Empty the mind. Then repeat the power statement.

Before sleep you enter alpha by default. What you repeat there is what the night installs.

Sit with thisWhat did you rehearse last night — the worry, or the statement?

The identity

Executive mode as home

Repeat the move until it is not a morning trick. It is who you are when nothing special is happening.

Rewiring identity is not a speech in the mirror. It is a nervous system that defaults to choice instead of chase. Science gives you the levers: state, attention, one action. Spirit gives you the reason: you are allowed to create a life instead of surviving one.

When force appears, you have left the move. Notice the feeling. Make the one move it is asking for.
