The 5 Unshakable Moves.

The Unshakable Mind is a 9-step, science-backed daily protocol. But you never carry all nine. You notice how you feel, and the feeling tells you which of the 5 Unshakable Moves to make. One feeling, one move, that's all you need today.

"I'm tense and on edge."
Calm down
00 Prime: breath, power statement, and movement to regulate before you plan.
"My head is too full."
Empty the mind
01 Empty · 02 Sort: everything out of your head, then sorted until the chaos has a shape.
"I don't know what matters."
Choose what matters
03 Vision · 04 Decide: a concrete picture of the day, won, and three priorities tied to purpose.
"I keep getting pulled away."
Protect it
05 Protect · 06 Execute: blocked time, phone out of the room, one thing at a time.
"I'm spiraling, it feels heavy."
Return to now
07 Interrupt the Loop · 08 Close: "Can I control this?", then release the day before sleep.
